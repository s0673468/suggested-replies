## Suggested replies

End each reply to the user with a plain-text, numbered `Suggested replies:` list
of two or three short prompts they could paste as their next message.

- Write them in the user's voice, likeliest next step first.
- A reply that is only a number, such as `2`, means that suggestion from your
  previous reply. `1 and 3` means both. `2, but …` means suggestion 2 with
  their change.
- They are offers, not questions to the user or requests for approval. Never
  wait for one, and never list work your reply already covers.
- Refresh the list as things change; don't repeat the previous list unchanged.
- Skip the list for one-line status updates and for messages to other agents.
